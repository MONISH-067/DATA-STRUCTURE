=== 2. Height of a B-tree of order 100 holding 5,000,000 keys (formulae) ===
order m=100: at most 99 keys and 100 children per node; at least ceil(m/2)=50 children (min degree t=50) except the root
maximum height h (edges) for n keys: h <= log_t((n+1)/2) = log_50(2500000.5) = 3.766 -> h <= 3 (at most 4 levels = 4 disk reads)
minimum height: n <= m^(h+1) - 1  ->  h+1 >= log_100(5000000+1) = 3.349 -> at least 4 levels
so every search reads exactly 4 nodes at worst, i.e. height 3 edges / 4 levels; binary tree: log2(5e6) = 22.25 -> about 23 reads
